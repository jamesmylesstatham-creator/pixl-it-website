Why it matters

A spreadsheet of licences and warranty dates isn't a system.

Most businesses end up with kit and software bought at different times, from different places, tracked nowhere in particular. That's how renewals get missed, warranties lapse unnoticed, and nobody remembers which laptop belongs to who.

We keep a single record of what you own, what it costs, and when it needs attention — so replacing a dead laptop or renewing a licence is a five-minute job, not a scramble.
