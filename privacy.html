Who we are

This website is operated by PIXL IT Limited ("PIXL IT", "we", "us"), a company registered in England & Wales (Company No. 15831363), registered office Bartle House, 9 Oxford Court, Manchester, M2 3WQ.

What we collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as when you contact us by email, phone, or a form on this site — typically your name, business name, email address, phone number, and the content of your message.

We also collect limited technical information automatically when you visit the site, such as pages viewed and general device/browser information, via the cookies described below.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — needed for the site to function (for example, remembering your cookie choice and your light/dark and accessibility preferences).
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how the site is used, so we can improve it. These are only set if you accept them.

You can change your cookie choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, provide quotes, deliver our services, and — where you've agreed to it — send you relevant updates. We don't sell your information to third parties.

Who we share it with

We may share information with trusted suppliers who help us run our business and deliver our services (for example, hosting, email, and accounting providers), and where required by law. Each supplier is only given the information they need to do their job.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet our legal obligations, and no longer than necessary after that.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you think we've handled your data incorrectly.
