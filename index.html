Common questions

Before you get in touch.

How fast do you respond when something breaks?

Critical issues — systems down, a suspected breach — get picked up immediately. Routine requests are typically handled the same working day.

Do you support Mac as well as Windows?

Yes. We support Windows, Mac, mobile devices, and the servers and network kit that sit behind them.

What's actually included in your support?

Monitoring, patching, backups and helpdesk support are all part of the same service, not bolt-on extras — so fixing a routine issue doesn't come with a surprise invoice.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

We keep agreements straightforward and reviewed regularly. Get in touch and we'll talk you through exactly what that looks like for your business.

How do we switch from our current IT provider?

We handle the handover — auditing what you've got, agreeing a cutover date, and making sure nothing falls through the cracks during the switch.

Do you work with businesses outside Manchester?

Most of our clients are in and around Manchester and the North West, but remote monitoring and support mean we work with businesses across the UK.
